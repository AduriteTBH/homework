Privacy & stored data
Signing in links your login to a Surfd account. Your username, verified times, splits, and successful command replays are public. Replays contain movement and view commands; they do not record your screen, microphone, or other websites.
The account service stores sign-in information. Name changes and moderator reasons are kept privately for moderators until the account is deleted. Server logs may include connection information for operation and abuse prevention. Local settings and local times stay in this browser. You can delete your account above.
Ranked runs use the published map and movement rules. Practice, restored positions, paused runs, and changed movement settings are not eligible. Automated runs may be removed from the public board.




Surfd ranks
How points work
Each map counts once: your best verified time. A completion is worth 100 points, plus up to 400 for your position on that map’s leaderboard. The performance part is 400 divided by the square root of your place, rounded down. Equal times earn equal points.
Tier 2 maps Reprise and Eclipse count double. Unrated maps use the baseline weight. Performance points can change when other players improve. Repeating a finish earns no extra points. Solo, EU and NA share the same ranks. Practice and unverified runs do not count.
